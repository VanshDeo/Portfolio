# My Engineering Journey

A chronological timeline of my learning, research investigations, and software engineering milestones.

---

## 2025 — Present

- **Edge AI & Embedded Inference**: Continued optimizations on `Swara`, reducing parameter footprints for INT8-quantised wake-word models running on low-power ARM architectures.
- **Space Science Research**: Continued work with solar wind ion telemetry from Aditya-L1's SWIS-ASPEX payload, identifying plasma characteristics linked to interplanetary halo CME events.
- **Developer Community Leadership**: Led technical workshops and onboarding sessions for junior developers at Kalyani Government Engineering College (KGEC).

---

## 2024

- **Zero-Knowledge Smart Contracts**: Prototyped `SealBid` on the Midnight Network, exploring privacy-preserving auction mechanisms and confidential state verification.
- **Computer Vision & Hardware Actuation**: Developed the `Hornet Detection and Automated Response` pipeline, integrating camera-based object detection with real-time physical barrier triggers on a Raspberry Pi.
- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation**: Built `Chorus`, a citation-backed knowledge synthesis platform utilizing Google Gemini API and vector embeddings.
- **Collegiate Competitions**: Participated in inter-college hackathons and developer sprints focusing on decentralized applications.

---

## 2023

- **Full-Stack & Systems Foundations**: Deepened expertise across TypeScript, Next.js, Python, and smart contract development in Solidity.
- **Open-Source Exploration**: Contributed to community utilities and began experimenting with model quantization and local AI deployment on embedded hardware.
